Before you ask

Quick answers

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No card, no contract. If we're a good fit, we'll share a proposal on the call. If not, you keep the audit and the three fixes.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. Many practices we audit have never run ads. We'll tell you whether ads make sense for you at all, or whether your directory profiles and website should come first.

Will you need access to my accounts?

Not for the audit. We review what's publicly visible: your profiles, your site, and the tracking scripts on its pages.

I'm licensed in more than one state. Does that matter?

Yes, in a good way. We'll look at demand in each state and where PSYPACT or counseling compact rules apply to you.
