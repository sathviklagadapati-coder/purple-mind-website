Ethics & privacy

Marketing that holds up in front of your licensing board.

Therapy marketing has rules most agencies don't know about. These are the ones we follow on every account.

No client testimonials

We never ask current or former clients for reviews or quotes. We build trust with your credentials, your approach, and clear information instead. APA Ethics Code 5.05 · ACA Code C.3.b · NASW 4.07

No tracking on sensitive pages

Ad pixels and session recorders stay off intake forms, booking pages, and condition-specific pages, so health information isn't shared with ad platforms. HHS OCR guidance on online tracking technologies

Business Associate Agreements

Any tool that could touch client information is covered by a signed BAA, or we don't use it. HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules

Only the states you're licensed in

Telehealth ads and landing pages are limited to the states where you can legally practice, and your site lists them plainly.

Crisis resources on every site

Every site we build shows the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and makes clear that online therapy isn't an emergency service.

FAQ

Questions practices ask us

Do you work with insurance-based practices?

Yes. We work with private-pay, in-network, and mixed practices. We'll shape your messaging and ad targeting around the panels you're on and the fees you charge.

Will you ever see my clients' information?

No. We set things up so client details go straight into your EHR or practice management system. We see counts, such as how many consults were booked, never names or notes.

How soon will I get new consult requests?

Paid ads usually bring the first requests within a few weeks of launch. Search and directory work builds more slowly, often over three to six months. We'll give you a realistic forecast after the audit.

I'm licensed in more than one state. Can you handle that?

Yes. We build state-specific pages and campaigns, and keep each one inside the rules for that state, including PSYPACT and counseling compact states where they apply.

Do I have to write the content myself?

No. We write it, and you approve it. Nothing clinical goes live without a licensed clinician on your team signing off.
