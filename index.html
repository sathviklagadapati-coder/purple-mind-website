Ethics & privacy

Marketing that holds up in front of your licensing board.

Therapy marketing has rules most agencies have never read. These five are non-negotiable on every account we run.

01

No client testimonials

We never solicit reviews or quotes from current or former clients. Trust comes from credentials, approach and clear information.APA 5.05 · ACA C.3.b · NASW 4.07

02

No tracking on sensitive pages

Ad pixels and session recorders stay off intake forms, booking pages and condition-specific pages.HHS OCR guidance on online tracking technologies

03

BAA on every tool

Any vendor that could touch client information signs a Business Associate Agreement, or we don't use it.HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules

04

Licensed states only

Telehealth ads and landing pages are limited to states where you can legally practice, and your site lists them plainly.

05

Crisis resources on every site

Every site shows the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and states clearly that online therapy is not an emergency service.

FAQ

Questions practices ask us

Do you work with insurance-based practices?

Yes. Private-pay, in-network and mixed. We shape messaging and targeting around the panels you're on and the fees you charge.

Will you ever see my clients' information?

No. Client details go straight into your EHR or practice management system. We see counts, such as how many consults were booked, never names or notes.

How soon will I get new consult requests?

Paid ads usually bring the first requests within a few weeks of launch. Search and directory work builds over three to six months. You'll get a realistic forecast after the audit.

I'm licensed in several states. Can you handle that?

Yes. We build state-specific pages and campaigns and keep each inside that state's rules, including PSYPACT and counseling compact states.

Do I have to write the content myself?

No. We write it, you approve it. Nothing clinical goes live without a licensed clinician on your team signing off.
